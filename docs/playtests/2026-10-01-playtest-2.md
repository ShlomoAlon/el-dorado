## Summary
About 80 minutes of a planned 4-hour session (2026-09-30 23:18 onward), in your Chrome at 1536×639. The build changed mid-session (6b6c2be → app.a589990fee.js); results hold on both. Five of last session's items are fixed, but the board layout on a normal laptop screen is badly broken, and most remaining jank has one cause with a one-line fix.

Top problems, most important first:
1. The board is fitted wrong on short laptop screens: small (625×306), off-centre, its right end under the market and its bottom 53 px under the hand. On Hills of Gold all four explorers start under the hand; on Witch's Cauldron El Dorado itself does.
2. Most jank is the static terrain being re-rasterised. Hiding only the terrain SVG cut slow frames 4–6×; giving it its own layer (#board{will-change:transform}) cut them 2–5× in play and to zero when stepping a replay.
3. Pass-and-play leaks hands: during an AI's turn, the previous human's newly drawn hand is face-up while the device is handed to the next player.
4. The board shrinks and wanders during play: the recap box swings between 144 and 341 px tall, and the board re-fits smaller, jumps between turns and slides under the recap.
5. Returning players see a blank screen for 1.2–1.6 s: first content waits on /api/config then /api/me, one after the other.
6. Reloading into a running online game shows the room lobby for over a second (it fades fully in, then out).
7. Dead-end purchases: you can start buying a card you can't afford and only get out with Cancel.
8. The same local game in two tabs silently overwrites the other tab's progress.
9. Landscape phone is unplayable (End turn and Undo sit on the board); phone portrait cuts names to one letter and pushes cards off-screen.
10. Joining with a mistyped room code shows "Connection lost. Reconnecting…" for about 23 s before saying the room may have closed.

## Jank and performance
Frame rate is no better than last session (2.7–2.9% of frames over 34 ms across whole games), and the main cause is now pinned down: the static terrain SVG is re-rasterised during play.

Root cause. #stage is one composited layer holding the terrain (~1,940 elements), the overlay SVG and the moving pieces. Any highlight, trail or piece change repaints the whole layer, terrain included. I ran interleaved two-round segments in fresh local games, hiding one part at a time, then tried the fix in-page:
- The one-line fix #board{will-change:transform} gave 0.6% and 1.2% slow frames vs 2.9% and 2.4% normal in play. Stepping a replay 12 times: 4 then 0 slow frames (worst 33 ms) vs 14 normal (worst 98 ms). Check it against your zoom-sharpness and memory tests before shipping.
- Turn hand-offs still cost 100–117 ms frames; from about round 11 most AI turns have a 115–184 ms frame. Every replay step costs a 50–100 ms frame (no script time).
- Local AI thinks on the main thread: 15 frames in one game were pure script inside the AI timer (aiChoose), 52–183 ms each, mostly Fawcett. Each one freezes whatever is animating.
- Hidden history column: in a replay the page had 12,378 DOM nodes; the left "every turn" list held 8,159 of them (32 turns × ~255 nodes) while hidden with display:none, still kept up to date.
- One-off hitches: opening the menu 66–148 ms (once 331 ms with 121 ms of forced layout); opening All cards 273 ms; opening a replay 167 + 137 ms; every window resize 99–198 ms; each zoom-button step ~49 ms.
- Ruled out: CSS filters and shadows (2.4% vs 2.3%, 1.6% vs 1.5% with and without), the recap panel, the moving pieces, the replay evaluation panel. Idle is a flat 60 fps; pan and wheel zoom are smooth.

## Board fit and layout on desktop
At 1536×639 every board is squeezed to 306 px tall, pushed right, and partly hidden under the hand and the market.
- Hand reserve is 53 px too small. The fit assumes the hand starts at y=524 (camera.js:39, b = H − cw·1.4·0.62), but the hand's top edge is at y=471. The bottom 53 px of every board sits under the hand.
  - Hills of Gold: all four explorers start under the hand (pins at y 464–506). Board 438×306.
  - Witch's Cauldron: El Dorado (the goal) is under the hand. Board 381×306, hexes about 12 px.
  - First Expedition and Winding Paths: bottom rows hidden; pieces visible.
- Off-centre and under the market. At this height the scale (0.369) trips fit()'s "too small to play (phones)" branch (camera.js:52-55). It recentres on the current explorer but can't zoom in, so the board just shifts about 260 px right, its El Dorado end under the market. Fit lands somewhere different each time. 1366×768, 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 2560×1080 fit fine.
- Fit is inconsistent between games: one 3-player pass-and-play game fitted large (about 800×315); the AI games at the same size did not.
- Recap height swings every turn: 144 → 341 → 144 → 241 px. At 341 px it covers over half the screen. The board re-fits smaller when the recap grows and never grows back (454×222 by round 8, hexes about 15 px).
- The board wanders and teleports: x 470→481→427→351→221→145 over rounds, at y=122 its top under the recap. A camera watch saw a 183 px jump in a single frame at the start of my turn.
- Empty boxes: the recap is an empty 400×143 panel on turn 1; the prompt row above it is blank on your own turn; with History on the left the prompt is an empty bordered pill.
- Overlaps: the pay slot covers the recap and the board; the market hover preview covers Menu and clips its count badge; the All cards close button sits exactly on Menu; a toast lands on the recap; the aim arrow runs from your explorer across the recap and market to wherever the pointer is.
- Live resize: each resize costs a 99–198 ms frame, and returning to 1280×720 put the board 40 px from where it started.
- History "every turn on the left" gives a much better layout (no recap block, board large and centred), which supports the diagnosis.
- The Start expedition button is 293 px below the visible part of the menu at this size, and not sticky.

## Bugs and broken behaviour
These are wrong outcomes, not just rough edges.
- Pass-and-play privacy leak. Ana, Ben and Raleigh (AI) with hand hiding on: when Ben ends his turn, Raleigh plays with the cover off and Ben's newly drawn hand face-up, exactly while the device goes to Ana. Seen twice.
- Dead-end purchases. Treasure Chest (cost 3, no "Can buy" label) with 2.5 coins in hand still opens a purchase; with every card paid it shows "2½ / 3" and only Cancel works. The same happens when a card was affordable before you moved and isn't after.
- Same local game in two tabs. Both resume it, then overwrite each other's save; whichever writes last wins and the other tab's progress is lost. No warning, no cross-tab sync.
- Profile rank vs Leaderboard. Profile says rank #5; the Leaderboard lists me 4th of 4. The leaderboard filters retired AIs, the profile's rank count doesn't. The API also returns rating 1193.9000000000005.
- Wrong room code. About 23 s (9 retries) of "Connection lost. Reconnecting…" before "Could not reach this room. It may have closed." The bad code stays in the URL, so a reload repeats it.
- AI seats wiped by choosing a course. Humboldt and Fawcett become Human when you pick Hills of Gold (Start stays enabled), and switching back to First Expedition doesn't restore them.
- 2-player seat dropdowns offer AIs that silently snap back to Human.
- Online first turn is short: in a 60 s room my first turn opened at 0:47; in a 90 s room at 1:02. Later turns start full.
- AI network file isn't versioned. /ai/first.bin (331 KB) is served uncompressed with max-age=0, must-revalidate. A tab left open across a deploy (one happened during this session) loads the new network into old code.
- Saved by an older build: a pass-and-play game saved by the previous build resumed with hand hiding off (its record had no privacy field); new saves include it.
- Not reproduced: one production assertion ("the removal choice shows exactly when the game asks for one") fired several times online, but only while my early bot was tapping cards during a pending move. A full game with a cleaner bot showed no errors.

## Gameplay flow and friction
A typical turn takes 6–7 actions (move: tap card + tap space, or one drag; buy: tap the card + tap each coin; end: one click, two with leftovers). Where it rubs:
- Nothing announces your turn in a local game: the banner is gone and the prompt is blank when idle, so only the chip outline and End turn changing tell you.
- Keeping a card takes 3 clicks: End turn → Keep all (only marks the cards; the button flips to "Keep none") → End turn.
- Double-click risk: End turn and Discard & end turn share one spot, so a double-click discards without showing the choice. In pass-and-play, Reveal hand sits where Undo appears.
- Rubble and blockades need an extra Confirm even when exactly the required cards are picked (last build moved at once). The instruction appears twice (prompt and hex tooltip), as it does for Scientist (prompt and a centre banner).
- Silent drops: a card tapped while your explorer is still walking is ignored; tapping another card while a multi-space card has strength left switches without saying the leftover is lost.
- Noise in targets: spaces behind you (further from El Dorado) stay highlighted after rubble and multi-space moves.
- Unusable cards look usable: a Sailor with no reachable water isn't dimmed; tapping it says "No space this card reaches."
- Hand re-flows: paid cards leave gaps until the purchase completes; picking a card to remove shifts the others by ~21 px.
- Start a new game from the in-game menu still discards the running game without asking.
- Menu doesn't pause local AIs: 7 AI moves happened behind a menu open for 6 s.
- 2-player: "Tap a pawn to switch" disappears the moment you select a card, which is when you'd switch; pawns are about 20 px at the default fit.
- Results don't explain order: non-arrivals ranked by distance show only "Still in the jungle", including "Humboldt wins" with nobody arrived. Your own chip has no "left" mark after resigning.
- Hard to read: recap and history are tiny card images with fragments ("1space", "6spaces" with the space missing, "base camp", "›"); the replay evaluation heading "Fawcett's turn for Ana" is cryptic; card titles are cut on the cards themselves ("Jack of All Trad…").
- Rules: unchanged, 644 words, Online and AI sections before the turn rules, no pictures or tutorial.
- AI: only First Expedition with 3–4 players; waiting for 3 AIs takes 9–13 s a round; Humboldt walked back and forth over a base camp for several rounds, which looks like dithering.
- Keyboard: Esc and Ctrl+Z work; Tab starts at the zoom buttons; cards and spaces can't be focused, so no keyboard play.
- Works well: drag to move, Native, joker (offers every symbol, no extra choice), multi-space moves with "2 machetes left" and Stop moving, blockade claim, Transmitter pick, Scientist with Remove/Skip, Undo of a purchase, resume after an online detour.

- Rejoin flash: on reload the room lobby fades fully in (337–549 ms), stays until 1,152 ms and closes at 1,373 ms, for a game already under way.
- Time bank still uncapped: in a 90 s room my clock read 1:02, 2:30, 3:59, 5:28, 6:56, 8:24 at the start of turns 1–6.
- Timeout: works (red from 0:09, turn passes at 0:00) but there's no "you ran out of time" message, and the prompt keeps showing my red 0:00 through the AI turns.
- Quick match alone: "Start now" stays disabled until a second person joins; no count of searching players, no estimate, no AI fill (the Random course quick match uses doesn't allow AI).
- Room lobby: each Add AI pushes the AI buttons down, so clicking the same spot twice added Raleigh, then Fawcett. The lobby's Rated toggle seems to carry into the Create form's default.
- Re-opening a game you resigned from shows you as if still seated (your hand, all buttons disabled) with no "you left · watching" message.
- Errors: with the network failing, Leaderboard and Profile show stale data with no hint, and Create room shows the raw browser text "Failed to fetch".
- "My games" still reshuffles: 7 local rows at 15 ms, then 17 rows re-sorted at 1.1 s.
- Works: the tab title shows "● Your turn"; two tabs in the same room stay in sync; a full 4-player online game (20 rounds) ran with 2.9% slow frames and no errors; Copy link, colour choice and the Rated toggle all work.

## Method and caveats
Played in your own Chrome through the Claude extension: about 6 local games (3- and 4-player vs AI, pass-and-play, 2-player hot-seat on all five courses) and about 10 online rooms, all private and unrated except one quick-match lobby left before it started.
- Frame numbers come from a frame recorder and Long Animation Frame entries in the page. Clicks made through the extension inflate frame times, so performance was re-measured through the page's own calls or during AI turns with no input.
- Early numbers taken while the Chrome window was covered were discarded and re-measured with full control.
- A greedy bot played some of my turns to reach late-game states quickly; findings it could have caused were re-checked by hand or dropped.
- Not tested: real touch input, reduced motion, a first visit with an empty cache, two different people in one online room, sign-in and sign-out.
